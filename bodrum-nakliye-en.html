Frequently Asked Questions

Which moving/transport services do you offer in Bodrum?

We offer household goods, holiday-home goods, single-item, office/workplace, boat/yacht equipment, tradesman/e-commerce goods, urgent and intercity transport services.

Can you give a fixed price for transport?

No, the price depends on distance, floor/elevator access, quantity of items and seasonal demand; message us on WhatsApp for an exact quote.

Is your transport insured?

Yes, we provide insured transport.

Can you move items on streets closed to vehicle traffic?

No, we cannot serve areas closed to vehicle traffic (pedestrian zones, etc.); transport has to be door-to-door.

Which areas of Bodrum do you serve?

We serve the whole Bodrum peninsula; check our region-based courier pages for your specific area.

I'm not sure which transport page I need — what should I do?

Just tell us your situation by phone or WhatsApp; we'll work out the right service and vehicle together.
