Frequently Asked Questions

How do I request a courier in Bodrum?

Reach us by phone or WhatsApp with the pickup address, delivery address and the type of item. Adding the neighborhood name and a location pin speeds things up, since street names can repeat in Bodrum.

Which areas of Bodrum do you serve?

Bodrum Merkez, Gümbet, Bitez, Akyarlar, Ortakent, Müskebi, Yahşi, Turgutreis, Gümüşlük, Yalıkavak, Gündoğan, Gölköy, Türkbükü, Torba, Güvercinlik, Konacık and Çırkan, among others. Message us for an address not listed and we'll confirm availability.

Do you deliver to areas far from the center, like Yalıkavak or Türkbükü?

Yes. Yalıkavak is about 18 km from the district center and Türkbükü about 15 km; price and time depend on the distance between the pickup and delivery addresses, so share both for an exact answer.

Can you deliver to a marina, yacht or boat?

Yes — share the marina name (Milta Bodrum, Yalıkavak Marina, D-Marin Turgutreis, etc.), the pier/pontoon, the boat name and the recipient's phone number.

How are price and delivery time determined?

Both depend on the distance between the pickup and delivery addresses, the priority (urgent or standard) and the type of item; message the addresses on WhatsApp for an exact quote.

Is cash on delivery available?

Yes, cash or card payment can be collected at the moment of delivery — see our cash-on-delivery courier page for details.
